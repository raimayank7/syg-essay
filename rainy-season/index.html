Essay on Rainy Season

Three ready essays on Rainy Season in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on Rainy Season

For classes 1 to 3 · 88 words

  1. The rainy season comes after summer.
  2. In India it begins in June and lasts till September.
  3. The sky is covered with dark clouds.
  4. It rains heavily and the weather becomes cool.
  5. Trees and plants look fresh and green.
  6. Farmers are happy because rain is good for their crops.
  7. Children love to play in the rain and float paper boats.
  8. Sometimes we see a rainbow in the sky.
  9. We should not let water collect near our house, because mosquitoes breed in it.
  10. I like the rainy season very much.

Short essay on Rainy Season

For classes 4 to 6 · 129 words

The rainy season, also called the monsoon, is the most awaited season in India. It comes after the burning heat of summer, usually in June, and stays till September. Dark clouds gather, cool winds blow, and the first showers fill the air with the sweet smell of wet earth. Ponds and rivers fill up, and trees and fields turn green. Farmers welcome the rain most of all, because crops such as rice depend on it. Children float paper boats and splash in puddles. Peacocks dance and frogs croak. The season has its problems too. Roads get waterlogged, and stagnant water breeds mosquitoes that spread dengue and malaria. Too much rain brings floods. Still, the monsoon is the lifeline of our country, and we should store its water with care.

Long essay on Rainy Season

For classes 7 to 10 · 375 words

India has many seasons, and each has its own charm. But none is welcomed with as much joy as the rainy season. After the long, hot months of April and May, when the earth is cracked and every creature is thirsty, the monsoon arrives like a blessing.

The monsoon winds blow in from the sea and reach Kerala around the beginning of June. Over the next few weeks they spread across the country. The season lasts till about September. The sky turns grey with clouds. There is thunder and lightning, and then the rain comes down, sometimes as a drizzle and sometimes in sheets.

The first rain is a treat for all the senses. The dry soil gives out a sweet smell. The dust is washed off the leaves, and everything looks freshly painted. In a few days, grass covers the bare ground. Ponds, lakes and wells fill up. Frogs begin to croak, and the peacock spreads its feathers and dances. If the sun comes out during a shower, a rainbow stretches across the sky.

For children, the rainy season means paper boats, puddles and hot pakoras. For farmers, it means life itself. A large part of Indian farming depends on the rain. Paddy, maize, cotton and many other crops are sown in this season. A good monsoon brings a good harvest, more income for villages and lower food prices. A weak monsoon can mean drought and hardship. That is why the whole country watches the clouds.

The season is not without trouble. Low-lying streets are flooded, and traffic crawls. Old houses leak. Rivers in spate can flood villages and destroy crops. Dirty, standing water breeds flies and mosquitoes, and diseases such as malaria, dengue, cholera and typhoid spread. We must drink boiled or filtered water, eat fresh food, and not allow water to collect in coolers, pots and tyres around the house.

Rainwater is precious. If we let it run off into drains, we lose it. If we collect it through rainwater harvesting and keep our ponds clean, it serves us for the rest of the year.

With its music of raindrops and its carpet of green, the rainy season refreshes the land and the heart. It is rightly called the queen of seasons.

वर्षा ऋतु पर 10 पंक्तियाँ

10 lines on Rainy Season in Hindi

  1. वर्षा ऋतु गर्मी के बाद आती है।
  2. भारत में यह जून में शुरू होती है और सितंबर तक रहती है।
  3. आकाश काले बादलों से ढक जाता है।
  4. तेज़ बारिश होती है और मौसम ठंडा हो जाता है।
  5. पेड़-पौधे हरे-भरे और ताज़ा दिखने लगते हैं।
  6. किसान खुश होते हैं क्योंकि बारिश उनकी फ़सलों के लिए अच्छी होती है।
  7. बच्चे बारिश में खेलते हैं और कागज़ की नाव चलाते हैं।
  8. कभी-कभी आकाश में इंद्रधनुष दिखाई देता है।
  9. घर के आस-पास पानी जमा नहीं होने देना चाहिए, क्योंकि उसमें मच्छर पनपते हैं।
  10. मुझे वर्षा ऋतु बहुत अच्छी लगती है।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. Arrival of the monsoon after summer
  2. Sights, sounds and smells of the first rain
  3. Importance for farmers
  4. Problems: floods, waterlogging, diseases
  5. Saving rainwater; why you like the season
