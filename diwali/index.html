Essay on Diwali

Three ready essays on Diwali in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on Diwali

For classes 1 to 3 · 81 words

  1. Diwali is the festival of lights.
  2. It comes in October or November every year.
  3. People clean and decorate their homes before Diwali.
  4. We light diyas and candles in the evening.
  5. We make rangoli at the door of our house.
  6. We worship Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Ganesha.
  7. People wear new clothes and share sweets.
  8. Diwali reminds us of the return of Lord Rama to Ayodhya.
  9. We should celebrate a clean and safe Diwali.
  10. I love Diwali because the whole family comes together.

Short essay on Diwali

For classes 4 to 6 · 122 words

Diwali, also called Deepawali, is one of the biggest festivals of India. It falls in October or November, on the new moon night of the month of Kartik. People believe that on this day Lord Rama came back to Ayodhya after fourteen years in the forest, and the people of the city lit rows of lamps to welcome him. Before Diwali, we clean our homes and decorate them with lights, flowers and rangoli. In the evening we worship Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Ganesha, light diyas and share sweets with neighbours and friends. Diwali teaches us that light wins over darkness and good wins over evil. It is a festival of joy, and it is best enjoyed in a safe and clean way.

Long essay on Diwali

For classes 7 to 10 · 296 words

Diwali, or Deepawali, is known as the festival of lights. The word Deepawali means a row of lamps. It is celebrated all over India and by Indians across the world, usually in October or November. For children, it is the happiest time of the year: schools are closed, the house smells of sweets, and every street shines at night.

There is a well-loved story behind the festival. Lord Rama returned to Ayodhya with Sita and Lakshmana after fourteen years of exile and after defeating Ravana. The people of Ayodhya lit earthen lamps in every home to welcome them. Since then, lamps are lit every year on this night. Many people also worship Goddess Lakshmi on Diwali and pray for the well-being of the family.

Diwali is not a festival of one day. The celebrations go on for five days: Dhanteras, Chhoti Diwali, the main day of Lakshmi Puja, Govardhan Puja and Bhai Dooj. Preparations begin weeks earlier. Homes are cleaned and painted. Markets are full of diyas, lights, clothes and gifts. On the main evening, families make rangoli, light diyas, offer prayers and then visit relatives and neighbours with sweets.

In recent years, people have become more aware of the harm done by loud and smoky firecrackers. They pollute the air, frighten animals and birds, and trouble old people and babies. Many families now choose to light more lamps and burst fewer crackers, or none at all. Giving sweets or clothes to those who have less is another beautiful way to celebrate.

Diwali carries a simple message: light is stronger than darkness, and goodness is stronger than evil. When we light a diya, we can also promise to remove a little darkness from our own habits. That is the true spirit of the festival of lights.

10 lines on Diwali in Hindi

  1. दीवाली रोशनी का त्योहार है।
  2. यह हर साल अक्टूबर या नवंबर में आती है।
  3. दीवाली से पहले लोग अपने घरों की सफ़ाई करते हैं।
  4. शाम को हम दीये और मोमबत्तियाँ जलाते हैं।
  5. हम घर के दरवाज़े पर रंगोली बनाते हैं।
  6. इस दिन माँ लक्ष्मी और गणेश जी की पूजा होती है।
  7. लोग नए कपड़े पहनते हैं और मिठाइयाँ बाँटते हैं।
  8. इसी दिन भगवान राम चौदह वर्ष बाद अयोध्या लौटे थे।
  9. हमें साफ़ और सुरक्षित दीवाली मनानी चाहिए।
  10. दीवाली मुझे बहुत अच्छी लगती है।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. What the festival is and when it comes
  2. The story of Lord Rama's return to Ayodhya
  3. Preparations: cleaning, shopping, rangoli
  4. The five days and the main evening
  5. A clean, safe Diwali and its message
