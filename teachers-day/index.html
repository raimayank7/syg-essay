Essay on Teachers' Day

Three ready essays on Teachers' Day in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on Teachers' Day

For classes 1 to 3 · 90 words

  1. Teachers' Day is celebrated on 5 September every year.
  2. It is the birthday of Dr Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan.
  3. He was a great teacher and the second President of India.
  4. On this day we thank our teachers.
  5. Students give flowers and cards to their teachers.
  6. In many schools, senior students take classes for a day.
  7. Teachers give us knowledge and show us the right path.
  8. A teacher is like a candle that burns to give light to others.
  9. We should respect and obey our teachers.
  10. I am thankful to all my teachers.

Short essay on Teachers' Day

For classes 4 to 6 · 120 words

Teachers' Day is celebrated in India on 5 September. It is the birthday of Dr Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, a great teacher, scholar and the second President of India. When some of his students and friends wished to celebrate his birthday, he asked that the day be observed as Teachers' Day instead. Since then, students across the country honour their teachers on this day. They bring flowers and hand-made cards, give speeches and put up programmes. In many schools, the senior students dress up as teachers and take the classes for a day. Teachers do much more than finish the syllabus. They shape our character and help us find our way in life. Teachers' Day is our chance to say thank you.

Long essay on Teachers' Day

For classes 7 to 10 · 309 words

In India, 5 September is celebrated as Teachers' Day. The day belongs to those who teach us to read, to think and to live well. It is also the birthday of Dr Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, who was born on 5 September 1888.

Dr Radhakrishnan was a philosopher and a much loved teacher. He taught at universities in India and abroad before he became the first Vice-President and then the second President of India. It is said that when he became President, some of his students and friends asked to celebrate his birthday. He replied that he would be proud if the day were observed as Teachers' Day. From 1962 onwards, the country has followed his wish.

On this day, schools take on a festive look. Students decorate the classrooms and greet their teachers with flowers, cards and small gifts made by hand. There are speeches, songs and funny plays in the assembly. The best part, for many of us, is that senior students become teachers for a day. They wear saris or formal clothes, carry registers and take the lower classes. By the end of the day, they understand how hard it is to keep forty children quiet and interested.

A teacher's work does not end with the lesson in the book. A good teacher notices the child who is sad, encourages the one who is weak, and corrects the one who is going wrong. Parents give us life, but teachers help us make something of it. Many great people have said that they owe their success to one teacher who believed in them.

The best gift for a teacher is not a card or a flower. It is a student who listens with attention, works honestly and grows into a good human being. On Teachers' Day, I thank my teachers and promise to try to be such a student.

10 lines on Teachers' Day in Hindi

  1. शिक्षक दिवस हर साल 5 सितंबर को मनाया जाता है।
  2. यह डॉ. सर्वपल्ली राधाकृष्णन का जन्मदिन है।
  3. वे एक महान शिक्षक और भारत के दूसरे राष्ट्रपति थे।
  4. इस दिन हम अपने शिक्षकों को धन्यवाद देते हैं।
  5. बच्चे अपने शिक्षकों को फूल और कार्ड देते हैं।
  6. कई स्कूलों में बड़े बच्चे एक दिन के लिए शिक्षक बनते हैं।
  7. शिक्षक हमें ज्ञान देते हैं और सही रास्ता दिखाते हैं।
  8. शिक्षक उस दीपक की तरह है जो खुद जलकर दूसरों को रोशनी देता है।
  9. हमें अपने शिक्षकों का आदर करना चाहिए।
  10. मैं अपने सभी शिक्षकों का आभारी हूँ।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. The date and whose birthday it is
  2. Who Dr Radhakrishnan was and how the day began
  3. How students celebrate in school
  4. What teachers do for us
  5. The best way to thank a teacher
