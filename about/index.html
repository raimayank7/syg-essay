About Essays

Essays is a free collection of school essays for classes 1 to 10. Every topic comes in three lengths, 10 lines, a short essay and a long essay, with 10 lines in Hindi and a list of points for writing your own.

Where the content comes from

  • Every essay on this site is our own original writing. Nothing is copied from textbooks, guide books or other websites.
  • Dates and facts in the essays (for example 15 August 1947, 26 January 1950, the launch of Swachh Bharat Abhiyan on 2 October 2014, the International Day of Yoga since 2015, India having 28 states and 8 union territories) are standard facts found in school textbooks and on Government of India websites. They were last checked on 11 November 2026. Facts that can change, such as the number of states or who holds an office, should be confirmed in your current textbook.
  • Proverbs and sayings used in the essays are traditional. Kabir's couplet is referred to in our own words, not quoted.

How to use these essays

Read them to collect ideas and words, then write in your own sentences. Essays about your mother, friend, school or family have blanks and example details: replace them with your own. Teachers can tell when an essay is copied, and your own honest essay will always score better.
