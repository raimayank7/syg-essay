    How to use this site well

    1. Read the essay once, aloud if you can. Use the Listen button to hear how the words are spoken.
    2. Look at the "Points" at the end of the page. They are the skeleton of the essay.
    3. Close the page and write the essay in your own sentences from the points.
    4. Compare, and borrow only the words or ideas you liked.

    New to essay writing? Read How to write an essay: planning, introduction, body, conclusion and common mistakes.

    Common questions

    Which essay should I use for my class?

    As a rough guide: 10 lines for classes 1 to 3, the short essay for classes 4 to 6, and the long essay for classes 7 to 10. Follow the word limit your teacher or question paper gives.

    Can I copy these essays for my homework?

    You may read and use them freely. It is better to write the points in your own sentences and add your own details. You will learn more, and your teacher will see the difference.

    Are the essays copied from books?

    No. Every essay here is written by us for this site.

    Do you have essays in Hindi?

    Every topic has 10 lines in Hindi at the end of its page.
