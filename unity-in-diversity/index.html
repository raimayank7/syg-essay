Essay on Unity in Diversity

Three ready essays on Unity in Diversity in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on Unity in Diversity

For classes 1 to 3 · 82 words

  1. India is a land of many religions, languages and cultures.
  2. People here wear different dresses and eat different food.
  3. Still, all of us are Indians.
  4. This is called unity in diversity.
  5. We celebrate Diwali, Eid, Christmas and Gurpurab together.
  6. We respect one another's customs.
  7. In times of trouble, the whole country stands together.
  8. Our freedom fighters came from every region and religion.
  9. Unity makes our country strong.
  10. We should live with love and never fight in the name of religion or language.

Short essay on Unity in Diversity

For classes 4 to 6 · 132 words

Unity in diversity means being one in spite of many differences. India is its finest example. In our country people follow different religions, speak different languages, eat different foods and celebrate different festivals. A person from Punjab and a person from Kerala may not understand each other's mother tongue, but both stand up for the same national anthem. We share the joy of Diwali, Eid, Christmas, Gurpurab, Pongal and Bihu. During the freedom struggle, Indians of every region and faith fought side by side. Whenever there is a flood, an earthquake or a war, help pours in from every corner. Our Constitution treats all citizens as equal. This unity is our strength, and we must protect it from those who try to divide us in the name of religion, caste or language.

Long essay on Unity in Diversity

For classes 7 to 10 · 385 words

A garden with only one kind of flower may be neat, but a garden with a hundred kinds is far more beautiful. India is such a garden. It holds an amazing variety of people, and yet it is one nation. This is what we mean by unity in diversity.

The diversity of India can be seen in every sphere. In geography, there are the icy Himalayas, the hot Thar desert, the rainy hills of the north-east and the palm-fringed coasts of the south. In religion, Hindus, Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, Buddhists, Jains and Parsis all live here, and many faiths were born on this soil. In language, the Constitution recognises twenty-two languages, and people speak hundreds of dialects. Food changes from makki ki roti in Punjab to dosa in Tamil Nadu and fish and rice in Bengal. Dresses, dances, music and festivals are just as varied.

What, then, holds us together? The first bond is a shared history and culture. The same epics, saints and stories are loved across the land. Pilgrim places in the four corners of the country have drawn people from every region for centuries. The second bond is the freedom struggle. People of all communities went to jail and gave their lives together, and that sacrifice made us one. The third bond is our Constitution. It gives the same rights to every citizen, whatever his or her religion, caste, language or state.

We see this unity in daily life. Neighbours of different faiths exchange sweets on festivals. Our cricket team, army and film industry have people from every part of India. When a natural disaster strikes one state, the others rush to help.

This unity cannot be taken for granted. From time to time, selfish people try to set one group against another in the name of religion, caste, language or region. Such quarrels weaken the country and stop its progress. History tells us that India lost its freedom when its rulers were divided.

As students, we can strengthen unity in simple ways. We can make friends with children from other states and faiths, learn a few words of another Indian language, respect every custom, and refuse to believe or forward messages that spread hatred.

Diversity is our beauty, and unity is our strength. Together they make India unique in the world.

10 lines on Unity in Diversity in Hindi

  1. भारत कई धर्मों, भाषाओं और संस्कृतियों का देश है।
  2. यहाँ के लोग अलग-अलग कपड़े पहनते हैं और अलग-अलग खाना खाते हैं।
  3. फिर भी हम सब भारतीय हैं।
  4. इसी को अनेकता में एकता कहते हैं।
  5. हम दीवाली, ईद, क्रिसमस और गुरुपर्व मिलकर मनाते हैं।
  6. हम एक-दूसरे के रीति-रिवाजों का सम्मान करते हैं।
  7. मुसीबत के समय पूरा देश एक साथ खड़ा होता है।
  8. हमारे स्वतंत्रता सेनानी हर क्षेत्र और हर धर्म से थे।
  9. एकता हमारे देश को मज़बूत बनाती है।
  10. हमें प्रेम से रहना चाहिए और धर्म या भाषा के नाम पर नहीं लड़ना चाहिए।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. Meaning; India as the best example
  2. Kinds of diversity: land, religion, language, food
  3. What unites us: culture, freedom struggle, Constitution
  4. Unity in daily life
  5. Dangers to unity; what students can do
