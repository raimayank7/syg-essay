Essay on My Favourite Teacher

Three ready essays on My Favourite Teacher in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 linesShort essayLong essayहिंदीPoints

Lines with a blank ( ______ ) are for your own details: fill in the name, class or number that is true for you, and change "she" or "he" where needed.

10 lines on My Favourite Teacher

For classes 1 to 3 · 71 words

  1. I like all my teachers, but ______ is my favourite teacher.
  2. She teaches us ______.
  3. She is kind and always smiling.
  4. She explains every lesson in an easy way.
  5. She tells us stories and gives examples.
  6. She never gets angry when we ask questions.
  7. She checks our notebooks carefully.
  8. She helps weak students after class.
  9. She teaches us to be honest and punctual.
  10. I want to be like her when I grow up.

Short essay on My Favourite Teacher

For classes 4 to 6 · 130 words

A good teacher can change the way a child looks at a subject. My favourite teacher did that for me. She teaches us mathematics, a subject I used to fear. She enters the class with a smile and begins with a small puzzle or a story. She explains every step on the board and asks us to try it ourselves. If we make a mistake, she does not scold; she asks us to find where we went wrong. She gives extra time to those who are slow and extra problems to those who are quick. She is strict about discipline and neat work, yet nobody is afraid of her. Because of her, I now enjoy mathematics. She has taught me that no subject is hard if we practise it regularly.

Long essay on My Favourite Teacher

For classes 7 to 10 · 343 words

Every teacher in my school works hard for us, and I respect them all. Still, there is one teacher whose period I wait for the whole day. She is my favourite teacher.

She is our class teacher and teaches us mathematics. She is always neatly dressed and reaches the class exactly on time. She knows each of us by name, and she seems to know our moods as well. If a child is unusually quiet, she notices and quietly asks what the matter is.

Her way of teaching is what makes her special. She never begins a new chapter straight from the book. First she asks a question from daily life: how much paint is needed for this wall, or how to share a pizza equally among six friends. Only when we are curious does she open the textbook. She writes clearly on the board, goes step by step and stops often to ask, "Any doubt?" No question is called silly in her class. Those who are shy are gently encouraged to come to the board.

She is fair to everyone. She does not have favourites. The topper and the weakest student get the same attention. For children who find the subject hard, she takes a short extra class during the games period once a week. She checks our notebooks carefully and writes a small remark of praise or advice on each one.

She is strict when it is needed. Late homework, untidy work and copying are not tolerated. But her anger never lasts and never hurts. She corrects the mistake, not the child.

She teaches us much more than mathematics. From her I have learnt to be punctual, to keep my work clean, to help a classmate without making fun of him or her, and to keep trying when a problem does not come out right the first time.

A teacher like her is rare. When I grow up, whatever work I do, I hope to do it with the same honesty and care that she brings to her class every day.

10 lines on My Favourite Teacher in Hindi

  1. मुझे अपने सभी शिक्षक अच्छे लगते हैं, पर ______ मेरी प्रिय शिक्षिका हैं।
  2. वे हमें ______ पढ़ाती हैं।
  3. वे दयालु हैं और हमेशा मुस्कुराती रहती हैं।
  4. वे हर पाठ को आसान तरीके से समझाती हैं।
  5. वे हमें कहानियाँ और उदाहरण सुनाती हैं।
  6. प्रश्न पूछने पर वे कभी नाराज़ नहीं होतीं।
  7. वे हमारी कॉपियाँ ध्यान से जाँचती हैं।
  8. वे कमज़ोर बच्चों की अलग से मदद करती हैं।
  9. वे हमें ईमानदार और समय का पाबंद बनना सिखाती हैं।
  10. बड़ा होकर मैं उनके जैसा बनना चाहता हूँ।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. Who the teacher is and what she or he teaches
  2. Appearance and nature
  3. Way of teaching
  4. Fairness, extra help and discipline
  5. What you have learnt beyond the subject
