Essay on My Aim in Life

Three ready essays on My Aim in Life in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on My Aim in Life

For classes 1 to 3 · 85 words

  1. Everyone should have an aim in life.
  2. A life without an aim is like a boat without a rudder.
  3. My aim in life is to become a doctor.
  4. A doctor serves sick people.
  5. I want to work in a village where there are few doctors.
  6. I will treat poor patients with care.
  7. To become a doctor, I must study science very well.
  8. I work hard at my studies every day.
  9. My parents and teachers encourage me.
  10. I will do my best to reach my aim.

Short essay on My Aim in Life

For classes 4 to 6 · 131 words

An aim gives direction to life. Without it, we drift like a boat without a rudder. Different people have different aims: some want to be engineers, some teachers, soldiers, players or artists. My aim is to become a doctor. I made up my mind when my grandmother was ill and a kind doctor in the government hospital treated her with great patience. I saw how much relief a good doctor brings to a worried family. I know that the road is long. I will have to study science seriously, clear a tough entrance examination and spend many years in training. I am ready for it. I have already made a timetable and I revise my lessons daily. When I become a doctor, I wish to serve where doctors are needed most.

Long essay on My Aim in Life

For classes 7 to 10 · 353 words

A person who starts a journey without knowing the destination reaches nowhere. In the same way, a student who studies without a goal wastes much effort. An aim tells us where to go, and it gives us the strength to keep going when the road is hard.

Choosing an aim needs thought. It should match our interest and our ability, not only the wishes of others or the hope of earning a lot of money. Some of my classmates dream of becoming pilots, cricketers, army officers or software engineers. I respect every honest profession. My own aim is to become a doctor.

The idea came to me two years ago. My grandmother fell seriously ill, and we took her to the district hospital. The doctor there was a young woman who had hardly any time to sit. Yet she spoke to every patient gently, explained the treatment in simple words and checked on my grandmother even late in the evening. My grandmother got well. That day I understood that a doctor does not treat only the illness; a doctor also removes fear.

Our country needs more doctors, especially in villages and small towns. People there often travel long distances for simple treatment. After completing my studies, I would like to work in such a place for at least some years. I do not want to run after money. A good doctor earns something better: the blessings of patients.

I know that wishing is not enough. To become a doctor, I must be strong in biology, chemistry and physics, pass a competitive entrance examination and then study medicine for many years. So I have started now. I follow a timetable, clear my doubts the same day, read about the human body in the library and keep myself fit.

I may face failures on the way. If I do, I will try again. And if life takes me to another profession, I will carry the same aim in a wider sense: to be useful to others. With hard work and the guidance of my parents and teachers, I am sure I will reach my goal.

10 lines on My Aim in Life in Hindi

  1. हर व्यक्ति के जीवन का एक लक्ष्य होना चाहिए।
  2. लक्ष्य के बिना जीवन बिना पतवार की नाव जैसा है।
  3. मेरे जीवन का लक्ष्य डॉक्टर बनना है।
  4. डॉक्टर बीमार लोगों की सेवा करता है।
  5. मैं ऐसे गाँव में काम करना चाहता हूँ जहाँ डॉक्टर कम हैं।
  6. मैं गरीब मरीज़ों का इलाज ध्यान से करूँगा।
  7. डॉक्टर बनने के लिए मुझे विज्ञान अच्छी तरह पढ़ना होगा।
  8. मैं रोज़ मन लगाकर पढ़ाई करता हूँ।
  9. मेरे माता-पिता और शिक्षक मेरा हौसला बढ़ाते हैं।
  10. मैं अपना लक्ष्य पाने के लिए पूरी मेहनत करूँगा।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. Why an aim is needed
  2. How to choose an aim; aims of others
  3. Your aim and what made you choose it
  4. What you will do after achieving it
  5. How you are preparing now
