Essay on My School

Three ready essays on My School in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

Lines with a blank ( ______ ) are for your own details: fill in the name, class or number that is true for you, and change "she" or "he" where needed.

10 lines on My School

For classes 1 to 3 · 78 words

  1. The name of my school is ______.
  2. It is one of the best schools in our town.
  3. My school has a big building and a large playground.
  4. The classrooms are airy and clean.
  5. There is a library full of good books.
  6. We have a computer lab and a science lab.
  7. Our teachers are kind and hard-working.
  8. We pray in the morning assembly every day.
  9. We study, play and take part in many activities.
  10. I am proud of my school.

Short essay on My School

For classes 4 to 6 · 126 words

My school is like a second home to me. It stands on the main road, a short walk from my house. It has a three-storey building, a wide playground and a small garden near the gate. The classrooms are bright, with big windows and neat rows of desks. We have a library, a computer room and a science laboratory. The day begins with the morning assembly, where we pray, sing the national anthem and hear the news of the day. Our teachers explain the lessons patiently and care about each child. Besides studies, we have games, music, art and a yearly sports day and annual function. My school has taught me discipline, friendship and good manners. I love my school and try to keep it clean.

Long essay on My School

For classes 7 to 10 · 351 words

A school is a temple of learning. It is the place where a child grows from a toddler holding a parent's finger into a confident young person. I have been studying in my school since nursery, and it has become a part of my life.

My school is in the middle of the town, away from the noise of the market. It has a large building painted in cream and brown, with a garden in front and a playground behind. As we enter the gate, we see a board with the thought for the day. There are more than thirty classrooms, each with a blackboard, a display board for our charts and big windows that let in light and air.

The school has all the facilities a student needs. The library has story books, magazines and reference books, and we get one library period every week. In the science laboratory we do simple experiments that make the textbook come alive. The computer room has enough computers for every child to practise. There is also a music room, an art room and a hall for functions.

The heart of a school is its teachers. Ours are well qualified, punctual and caring. They make sure that even the last bench understands the lesson. The principal knows many students by name and takes a round of the classes every day.

My school believes that studies alone do not make a complete person. So we have a games period, yoga, and clubs for debate, dance, science and gardening. We celebrate Independence Day, Republic Day, Teachers' Day and Children's Day. The annual sports day and the annual function are the two events we wait for all year.

Discipline is the first lesson here. We come on time, wear a clean uniform, stand in a line and keep the classroom tidy. These small habits will stay with us for life.

I owe a great deal to my school. It has given me knowledge, friends and good values. I am proud to be its student, and I hope that one day my school will be proud of me.

10 lines on My School in Hindi

  1. मेरे विद्यालय का नाम ______ है।
  2. यह हमारे शहर के सबसे अच्छे विद्यालयों में से एक है।
  3. मेरे विद्यालय की इमारत बड़ी है और खेल का मैदान भी बड़ा है।
  4. कक्षाएँ हवादार और साफ़ हैं।
  5. यहाँ एक पुस्तकालय है जिसमें अच्छी-अच्छी किताबें हैं।
  6. यहाँ कंप्यूटर कक्ष और विज्ञान प्रयोगशाला भी है।
  7. हमारे शिक्षक दयालु और मेहनती हैं।
  8. हम रोज़ सुबह प्रार्थना सभा में प्रार्थना करते हैं।
  9. हम यहाँ पढ़ते हैं, खेलते हैं और कई गतिविधियों में भाग लेते हैं।
  10. मुझे अपने विद्यालय पर गर्व है।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. Name and place of the school
  2. Building, classrooms, playground
  3. Library, labs and other facilities
  4. Teachers and principal
  5. Activities, discipline and what the school has given you
