Essay on Swachh Bharat Abhiyan

10 lines on Swachh Bharat Abhiyan

For classes 1 to 3 · 75 words

  1. Swachh Bharat Abhiyan means Clean India Mission.
  2. It was started on 2 October 2014.
  3. This date is the birthday of Mahatma Gandhi.
  4. Gandhiji wanted a clean India.
  5. The aim of the mission is to keep our country clean.
  6. It asks people to build and use toilets.
  7. We should throw waste only in dustbins.
  8. We should keep our home, school and streets clean.
  9. Cleanliness keeps diseases away.
  10. A clean India is the duty of every Indian.

Short essay on Swachh Bharat Abhiyan

For classes 4 to 6 · 136 words

Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, or the Clean India Mission, is a nationwide cleanliness campaign of the Government of India. It was launched by the Prime Minister on 2 October 2014, the birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi, who believed that cleanliness is next to godliness. The mission has two main aims: to end the practice of defecating in the open by building toilets for every household, and to keep streets, roads and public places free of garbage through proper collection and treatment of waste. A very large number of household and community toilets have been built in villages and towns under it. But no government can keep a country clean by itself. The mission will succeed only when every citizen stops littering and spitting, uses dustbins and toilets, and treats public places like his or her own home.

Long essay on Swachh Bharat Abhiyan

For classes 7 to 10 · 383 words

Mahatma Gandhi once said that sanitation is more important than independence. He himself cleaned the ashram toilets to show that no work is low. To honour his dream of a clean India, the Government of India launched the Swachh Bharat Abhiyan on 2 October 2014, his birth anniversary. The Prime Minister began the campaign by picking up a broom himself.

The need for such a mission was clear. Heaps of garbage on roadsides, choked drains, dirty railway stations and people relieving themselves in the open were common sights. Dirt is not only ugly. It spreads diarrhoea, cholera, typhoid and many other diseases, which kill children and keep families poor. Dirty surroundings also hurt tourism and the image of the country.

The mission has two parts, one for villages and one for cities. The first goal was to make India "open defecation free" by 2 October 2019, the 150th birth anniversary of Gandhiji. For this, the government gave money to poor families to build toilets at home, and community toilets were built in public places. The second goal is solid waste management: collecting garbage from every door, separating wet and dry waste, and recycling or treating it instead of dumping it. Cities are ranked every year in a cleanliness survey called Swachh Survekshan, which has created a healthy competition among them.

The campaign is also about changing habits. Film stars, players, students and ordinary citizens have joined cleanliness drives. Schools hold rallies and teach children to wash hands with soap. Many people now think twice before throwing a wrapper on the road.

Much has been achieved, but much remains. A toilet is useful only if it is used and kept clean. A dustbin helps only if we walk up to it. As students, we can do a great deal. We can keep our classroom and playground clean, carry our wrappers home if there is no bin, refuse plastic bags, and politely stop others from littering. We can keep separate bins for wet and dry waste at home.

A clean country is not built by sweepers alone. It is built by citizens who do not make it dirty in the first place. If one hundred and forty crore Indians take one step each towards cleanliness, the nation moves one hundred and forty crore steps forward.

10 lines on Swachh Bharat Abhiyan in Hindi

  1. स्वच्छ भारत अभियान का अर्थ है भारत को साफ़ रखने का अभियान।
  2. यह 2 अक्टूबर 2014 को शुरू किया गया था।
  3. यह दिन महात्मा गांधी का जन्मदिन है।
  4. गांधीजी स्वच्छ भारत चाहते थे।
  5. इस अभियान का उद्देश्य हमारे देश को साफ़-सुथरा रखना है।
  6. यह लोगों को शौचालय बनाने और उसका उपयोग करने के लिए कहता है।
  7. हमें कूड़ा केवल कूड़ेदान में डालना चाहिए।
  8. हमें अपना घर, स्कूल और गलियाँ साफ़ रखनी चाहिए।
  9. सफ़ाई से बीमारियाँ दूर रहती हैं।
  10. स्वच्छ भारत हर भारतीय की ज़िम्मेदारी है।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. Gandhiji's view of cleanliness; launch date
  2. Why the mission was needed
  3. Two goals: toilets and waste management
  4. Changing habits; public participation
  5. What students can do
