Essay on Value of Time

Three ready essays on Value of Time in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on Value of Time

For classes 1 to 3 · 78 words

  1. Time is very precious.
  2. Time once gone never comes back.
  3. We can earn lost money, but not lost time.
  4. We should do every work on time.
  5. A student should get up early and follow a timetable.
  6. We should not put off today's work till tomorrow.
  7. People who value time become successful.
  8. Lazy people waste time and feel sorry later.
  9. There is a saying: time and tide wait for none.
  10. We should make the best use of every minute.

Short essay on Value of Time

For classes 4 to 6 · 135 words

Time is the most valuable thing we have. Money that is lost can be earned again, and health that is lost can often be regained, but a minute that has passed will never return. That is why it is said that time and tide wait for none. Everyone, rich or poor, gets the same twenty-four hours each day. What we do with them decides our future. A student who follows a timetable, finishes each day's work the same day and does not waste hours on the phone has no fear of examinations. One who keeps saying "I will do it tomorrow" is left with regret. All great men and women were careful with their time. Valuing time does not mean working without rest. It means giving the right time to study, play, family and sleep.

Long essay on Value of Time

For classes 7 to 10 · 368 words

There is a well-known saying: "Time and tide wait for none." The waves of the sea do not stop for anyone, and neither does the clock. Time is free, but it is priceless. We cannot buy it, store it or borrow it. We can only use it or lose it.

Wealth can be earned again after a loss. A weak body can become strong with care. But a day that is wasted is gone for ever. A farmer who does not sow in season gets no crop. A passenger who is two minutes late misses the train. A student who idles through the year cannot make up in the last week. Kabir put it beautifully in a couplet that every Indian child knows: do tomorrow's work today, and today's work now.

Nature gives everyone the same amount of time: twenty-four hours a day. The difference between a successful person and an unsuccessful one lies in how those hours are spent. People who have done great things in science, sports, business or the arts were not given longer days. They simply did not waste what they had.

For students, time is especially precious, because habits formed now last for life. The best way to respect time is to make a timetable and follow it. Fix hours for study, play, meals and sleep. Do the hardest subject when the mind is fresh. Finish homework before play, not after. Keep the phone and television for a limited time. Above all, avoid the habit of postponing. "Later" easily becomes "never".

Being punctual is a part of valuing time. A person who is always late wastes not only his or her own time but also the time of others. Reaching school, a class or a meeting on time is a mark of respect.

Valuing time does not mean being busy every second. Rest, games and time with family are good uses of time. Wasting time means spending it on things that give neither joy nor benefit.

Childhood and youth come only once. If we use these years well, the rest of life becomes easier. Let us remember that every morning brings a fresh gift of twenty-four hours, and let us spend it wisely.

समय का महत्व पर 10 पंक्तियाँ

10 lines on Value of Time in Hindi

  1. समय बहुत कीमती है।
  2. बीता हुआ समय कभी वापस नहीं आता।
  3. खोया हुआ धन फिर कमाया जा सकता है, पर खोया हुआ समय नहीं।
  4. हमें हर काम समय पर करना चाहिए।
  5. विद्यार्थी को सुबह जल्दी उठना चाहिए और समय-सारणी के अनुसार चलना चाहिए।
  6. आज का काम कल पर नहीं टालना चाहिए।
  7. जो लोग समय की कद्र करते हैं, वे सफल होते हैं।
  8. आलसी लोग समय गँवाते हैं और बाद में पछताते हैं।
  9. कहावत है कि समय किसी का इंतज़ार नहीं करता।
  10. हमें हर पल का सही उपयोग करना चाहिए।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. Time cannot be bought or brought back
  2. Examples: farmer, train, student; Kabir's advice
  3. Everyone has the same 24 hours
  4. How a student should plan time
  5. Punctuality; rest is not waste
