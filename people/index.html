How to write these essays

There are two kinds of essays about people. For a great person, follow the order of the life: birth and childhood, education, main work, honours, death, and what we learn from that life. Facts and dates must be correct.

For someone you know, such as your mother, teacher or friend, facts are not enough. Show the person through small, true examples: what she says when you are late, how he helped you once. Our essays leave blanks for names so that you can put in your own. Change the details to match your real life; an honest essay always reads better than a copied one.
