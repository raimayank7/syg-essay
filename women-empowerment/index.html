Essay on Women Empowerment

Three ready essays on Women Empowerment in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on Women Empowerment

For classes 1 to 3 · 84 words

  1. Women empowerment means making women strong and independent.
  2. Women are half of our population.
  3. Girls have the same right to education as boys.
  4. Educated women can take their own decisions.
  5. Today women are doctors, pilots, soldiers, scientists and leaders.
  6. Women should get equal pay and equal respect.
  7. We should stop child marriage and dowry.
  8. Girls must feel safe at home, at school and on the road.
  9. When women move forward, the family and the country move forward.
  10. We should respect every girl and woman.

Short essay on Women Empowerment

For classes 4 to 6 · 128 words

Women empowerment means giving women the power to take their own decisions and equal chances in education, work and public life. Women are half of the population, and no country can progress by leaving half of its people behind. For a long time, girls were kept away from school and married early. Things are changing. Today Indian women are teachers, doctors, pilots, army officers, scientists, sportspersons and heads of companies. India has had a woman Prime Minister and women Presidents. The government runs schemes such as Beti Bachao Beti Padhao to save and educate the girl child. Still, problems such as dowry, unequal pay and violence remain. Real empowerment begins at home, when sons and daughters are given the same food, the same education and the same respect.

Long essay on Women Empowerment

For classes 7 to 10 · 376 words

A bird cannot fly with one wing. In the same way, a society cannot rise if only its men move ahead. Women empowerment means giving women the freedom, education and opportunities to shape their own lives and to take part fully in the life of the nation.

In ancient India, learned women were honoured. Over the centuries, however, the position of women fell. Girls were denied education, married in childhood and confined to the kitchen. Customs such as dowry treated them as a burden. Reformers like Raja Ram Mohan Roy, Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar, Jyotirao Phule and Savitribai Phule fought against these wrongs and opened the first doors of education for girls.

After independence, the Constitution gave women equal rights, including the right to vote, from the very first day. Since then Indian women have proved themselves in every field. Indira Gandhi became the Prime Minister. Pratibha Patil and Droupadi Murmu became Presidents of India. Kalpana Chawla travelled to space. Mary Kom and P. V. Sindhu won Olympic medals. Women fly fighter planes, run banks, lead scientific missions and win elections to panchayats, where seats are reserved for them.

Yet the picture is not complete. In many homes a son is still preferred over a daughter. Girls drop out of school to do housework or to be married early. Women are often paid less than men for the same work and carry the whole load of the home as well. Dowry, harassment and violence have not ended, and many women do not feel safe in public places.

The government has taken several steps: laws against dowry, child marriage and harassment at the workplace, the Beti Bachao Beti Padhao campaign started in 2015, scholarships and bicycles for schoolgirls, and savings and loan schemes for women. Self-help groups have made lakhs of village women earners.

Laws alone cannot change minds. Change must start in the family. Parents should give sons and daughters the same food, schooling and freedom, and teach sons to share housework and to respect women. Schools should encourage girls in science and sports. Each of us should speak up when a girl is teased or treated unfairly.

An educated, confident woman raises a healthy and educated family. When women are empowered, the whole nation is empowered.

10 lines on Women Empowerment in Hindi

  1. महिला सशक्तिकरण का अर्थ है महिलाओं को मज़बूत और आत्मनिर्भर बनाना।
  2. महिलाएँ हमारी आबादी का आधा हिस्सा हैं।
  3. लड़कियों को भी लड़कों के बराबर शिक्षा का अधिकार है।
  4. शिक्षित महिलाएँ अपने फ़ैसले खुद ले सकती हैं।
  5. आज महिलाएँ डॉक्टर, पायलट, सैनिक, वैज्ञानिक और नेता हैं।
  6. महिलाओं को बराबर वेतन और बराबर सम्मान मिलना चाहिए।
  7. हमें बाल विवाह और दहेज जैसी बुराइयों को रोकना चाहिए।
  8. लड़कियों को घर, स्कूल और सड़क पर सुरक्षित महसूस होना चाहिए।
  9. जब महिलाएँ आगे बढ़ती हैं तो परिवार और देश आगे बढ़ता है।
  10. हमें हर लड़की और महिला का सम्मान करना चाहिए।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. Meaning; why half the population cannot be left behind
  2. Position of women in the past; reformers
  3. Achievements of Indian women
  4. Problems that remain
  5. Steps by government; change that must begin at home
