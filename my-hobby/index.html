Essay on My Hobby

Three ready essays on My Hobby in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on My Hobby

For classes 1 to 3 · 75 words

  1. A hobby is something we do for joy in our free time.
  2. My hobby is gardening.
  3. I have a small garden in front of my house.
  4. I grow flowers and a few vegetables.
  5. I water the plants every morning and evening.
  6. I remove the weeds and dry leaves.
  7. It makes me happy to see a new flower.
  8. Gardening keeps me fresh and active.
  9. It teaches me patience and care.
  10. Everyone should have a good hobby.

Short essay on My Hobby

For classes 4 to 6 · 131 words

A hobby is an activity that we do in our free time because we enjoy it. People have different hobbies, such as reading, painting, dancing, collecting stamps or playing an instrument. My hobby is gardening. We have a small patch of land in front of our house and some pots on the terrace. I grow marigolds, roses, tulsi, mint and a few tomato plants. Every morning I water them before going to school. On Sundays I loosen the soil, remove the weeds and add compost made from kitchen waste. When a bud opens or a tomato turns red, I feel a joy that no video game gives me. Gardening has taught me patience, because a plant cannot be hurried. It also keeps me close to nature and away from the screen.

Long essay on My Hobby

For classes 7 to 10 · 331 words

After a long day of school and homework, the mind needs a change. Some people watch television or play on a phone. But the best rest comes from a hobby: an activity we choose ourselves and do out of love, not for marks or money.

Hobbies are of many kinds. Reading, drawing, singing, cooking, photography, cycling and collecting coins or stamps are all popular. My own hobby is gardening. I picked it up from my grandfather, who has looked after plants all his life.

We do not have a big garden. There is a strip of soil along the front wall of our house and about twenty pots on the terrace. In this small space I grow seasonal flowers such as marigold, petunia and sunflower, herbs such as tulsi, mint and coriander, and vegetables such as tomatoes, chillies and lady's finger.

My hobby has a routine. Every morning I water the plants and look at each one for a minute. A gardener learns to notice small things: a yellow leaf, an insect, a new bud. On Sundays I spend an hour or two on the bigger jobs. I loosen the soil, pull out the weeds, cut the dry twigs and add manure. We make our own compost from vegetable peels and dry leaves, so nothing is wasted.

Gardening has given me more than flowers. It has taught me patience, because a seed takes its own time. It has taught me responsibility, because plants suffer if I forget them for even two hot days. It has improved my science too: I have seen germination, pollination and the work of earthworms with my own eyes. And nothing tastes better than a tomato I have grown myself.

A good hobby refreshes the mind, uses our time well and sometimes even grows into a career. It keeps us away from bad habits and too much screen time. I believe every student should have at least one hobby and give it a little time every day.

10 lines on My Hobby in Hindi

  1. खाली समय में खुशी के लिए जो काम किया जाता है, उसे शौक कहते हैं।
  2. मेरा शौक बागवानी है।
  3. मेरे घर के सामने एक छोटा-सा बगीचा है।
  4. मैं उसमें फूल और कुछ सब्ज़ियाँ उगाता हूँ।
  5. मैं रोज़ सुबह-शाम पौधों को पानी देता हूँ।
  6. मैं घास-फूस और सूखे पत्ते हटाता हूँ।
  7. नया फूल खिलता देखकर मुझे बहुत खुशी होती है।
  8. बागवानी मुझे तरोताज़ा और चुस्त रखती है।
  9. यह मुझे धैर्य और देखभाल करना सिखाती है।
  10. हर किसी का कोई अच्छा शौक होना चाहिए।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. What a hobby is; kinds of hobbies
  2. Your hobby and how it began
  3. What exactly you do, and when
  4. What you have gained from it
  5. Why everyone needs a hobby
