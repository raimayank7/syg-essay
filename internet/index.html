Essay on Internet: Uses and Dangers

Three ready essays on Internet: Uses and Dangers in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on Internet: Uses and Dangers

For classes 1 to 3 · 87 words

  1. The internet connects computers and phones all over the world.
  2. We can find information on any subject in seconds.
  3. We can send emails and messages.
  4. Students can attend online classes.
  5. We can shop, pay bills and book tickets online.
  6. We can watch videos and listen to music.
  7. The internet has made the world a small place.
  8. But everything on the internet is not true.
  9. We should not share our personal details with strangers online.
  10. We should use the internet for good purposes and for a limited time.

Short essay on Internet: Uses and Dangers

For classes 4 to 6 · 133 words

The internet is a worldwide network that links millions of computers and phones. It has changed the way we learn, work, shop and talk to one another. With a few taps we can find information on any topic, read books, watch lessons, send emails and make video calls across the world. Banking, booking tickets, paying bills and even visiting a doctor can now be done online. During the lockdown, it was the internet that kept schools and offices running. However, the internet has a dark side. Not everything on it is true. People can waste hours on games and social media. Cheats steal money through fake links, and children can be bullied or misled by strangers. So we must use the internet carefully: check facts, protect our passwords and limit our time online.

Long essay on Internet: Uses and Dangers

For classes 7 to 10 · 424 words

If one invention has shrunk the world into a village, it is the internet. The internet is a huge network that connects computers, phones and other devices across the globe so that they can share information. What began as a small network for researchers is now used by billions of people every day.

The first great use of the internet is knowledge. Earlier, to find a fact we had to visit a library and turn the pages of an encyclopaedia. Now a search brings the answer in seconds, with pictures and videos. Students can watch a science experiment, hear the correct pronunciation of a word, or take a free course from a great university. Online classes have carried education to homes and villages where good teachers were not available.

The second use is communication. Emails, chat messages and video calls have made distance meaningless. A grandmother in a village can see her grandchild abroad every day. Offices hold meetings online, and people work from home.

The third use is services. We can open a bank account, send money, pay bills, file forms, book train and bus tickets, order food and groceries, and buy almost anything from our phone. Government services such as certificates and scholarships are available on portals. Small businesses and artists can reach customers across the country.

The internet is also a source of entertainment: music, films, games and sports are all a click away.

But the internet must be used with open eyes. First, anyone can post anything, so a great deal of what we read is false or one-sided. Rumours and fake news spread faster than truth. Second, it can become an addiction. Children and adults lose hours scrolling, and studies, sleep and health suffer. Third, there is crime. Fraudsters send fake messages and links to steal passwords and money. Personal photos can be misused. Children may face bullying or meet strangers who pretend to be friends. Fourth, there is content that is not fit for children.

A few simple rules keep us safe. Use the internet for a fixed time and a clear purpose. Check information from more than one trusted source. Never share passwords, OTPs, addresses or photos with strangers. Do not click on unknown links or download unknown files. Be as polite online as you are in person. Tell a parent or teacher at once if something online frightens or troubles you.

The internet is like fire. It can cook our food or burn our house. Used wisely, it is one of the greatest gifts of science.

10 lines on Internet: Uses and Dangers in Hindi

  1. इंटरनेट पूरी दुनिया के कंप्यूटरों और फ़ोनों को आपस में जोड़ता है।
  2. इससे हम किसी भी विषय की जानकारी कुछ ही पलों में पा सकते हैं।
  3. हम ई-मेल और संदेश भेज सकते हैं।
  4. विद्यार्थी ऑनलाइन कक्षाओं में पढ़ सकते हैं।
  5. हम ऑनलाइन खरीदारी कर सकते हैं, बिल भर सकते हैं और टिकट बुक कर सकते हैं।
  6. हम वीडियो देख सकते हैं और संगीत सुन सकते हैं।
  7. इंटरनेट ने दुनिया को छोटा बना दिया है।
  8. लेकिन इंटरनेट पर लिखी हर बात सच नहीं होती।
  9. हमें अनजान लोगों को अपनी निजी जानकारी नहीं देनी चाहिए।
  10. हमें इंटरनेट का उपयोग अच्छे कामों के लिए और सीमित समय तक करना चाहिए।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. What the internet is
  2. Knowledge and education
  3. Communication and services
  4. Dangers: false information, addiction, fraud
  5. Rules for safe use
