Essay on My Country India

Three ready essays on My Country India in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on My Country India

For classes 1 to 3 · 84 words

  1. India is my country.
  2. I am proud to be an Indian.
  3. New Delhi is the capital of India.
  4. Our national flag has three colours: saffron, white and green.
  5. The tiger is our national animal and the peacock is our national bird.
  6. People of many religions and languages live here together.
  7. The Himalayas stand in the north of India.
  8. The Ganga is our holy river.
  9. India is famous for its festivals, food and culture.
  10. I love my country and I will work for its progress.

Short essay on My Country India

For classes 4 to 6 · 128 words

India is my motherland, and I am proud of it. It is the seventh largest country in the world by area and has the largest population. It is also the world's largest democracy. New Delhi is its capital. In the north stand the snow-covered Himalayas; in the south the land meets the Indian Ocean. Great rivers such as the Ganga, Yamuna, Brahmaputra, Godavari and Kaveri make its plains fertile. India has 28 states and 8 union territories. Its people follow many religions, speak many languages and wear different dresses, yet they live as one nation. This is called unity in diversity. India is the land of Buddha, Guru Nanak and Mahatma Gandhi. Our national flag is the tricolour. I love my country and want to serve it honestly.

Long essay on My Country India

For classes 7 to 10 · 415 words

India, also called Bharat, is one of the oldest civilisations in the world. Cities of the Indus Valley flourished here thousands of years ago. It is the land where the Vedas were composed, where Buddha and Mahavira preached, and where great empires rose and fell. I am fortunate to be born here.

India lies in South Asia. It is the seventh largest country by area and, according to United Nations estimates, became the most populous country in 2023. The Himalayas guard it in the north. The Arabian Sea lies to the west, the Bay of Bengal to the east and the Indian Ocean to the south. Within these borders we find snow peaks, deserts, thick forests, fertile plains, plateaus and a long coastline. Rivers such as the Ganga, Yamuna, Brahmaputra, Narmada, Godavari, Krishna and Kaveri are its lifelines.

India became independent on 15 August 1947 and a republic on 26 January 1950. It is the largest democracy in the world: the people choose their government by voting. The country has 28 states and 8 union territories, with New Delhi as the capital. The President is the head of the state, and the Prime Minister is the head of the government.

Our national symbols fill us with pride. The national flag has three bands of saffron, white and green, with the Ashoka Chakra in the centre. "Jana Gana Mana", written by Rabindranath Tagore, is the national anthem, and "Vande Mataram" is the national song. The tiger is the national animal, the peacock the national bird and the lotus the national flower.

The real wealth of India is its variety. Hindus, Muslims, Sikhs, Christians, Buddhists, Jains and Parsis live here side by side. The Constitution lists twenty-two languages, and hundreds more are spoken. Food, dress, music and festivals change every few hundred kilometres. Yet in joy and in difficulty the country stands together. This unity in diversity is our greatest strength.

India has given the world zero and the decimal system, yoga and Ayurveda. Today it is known for its farmers, soldiers, scientists and engineers. It has sent spacecraft to the Moon and Mars, and it is one of the fastest growing large economies.

There are problems too: poverty, pollution, illiteracy and corruption. They will not be solved by the government alone. If every citizen is honest, keeps the surroundings clean, respects others and does his or her work well, India will be second to none. I love my country, and I promise to do my part.

10 lines on My Country India in Hindi

  1. भारत मेरा देश है।
  2. मुझे भारतीय होने पर गर्व है।
  3. नई दिल्ली भारत की राजधानी है।
  4. हमारे राष्ट्रीय ध्वज में तीन रंग हैं: केसरिया, सफ़ेद और हरा।
  5. बाघ हमारा राष्ट्रीय पशु है और मोर हमारा राष्ट्रीय पक्षी है।
  6. यहाँ कई धर्मों और भाषाओं के लोग मिल-जुलकर रहते हैं।
  7. भारत के उत्तर में हिमालय पर्वत है।
  8. गंगा हमारी पवित्र नदी है।
  9. भारत अपने त्योहारों, खान-पान और संस्कृति के लिए प्रसिद्ध है।
  10. मैं अपने देश से प्यार करता हूँ और इसकी उन्नति के लिए काम करूँगा।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. An ancient land; pride in being Indian
  2. Location, size, mountains, seas and rivers
  3. Democracy, states, capital
  4. National symbols
  5. Unity in diversity; achievements; our duty
