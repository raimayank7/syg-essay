Essay on Discipline

Three ready essays on Discipline in simple English: 10 lines for small classes, a short essay and a long essay, followed by 10 lines in Hindi and the points to write your own.

10 lines on Discipline

For classes 1 to 3 · 77 words

  1. Discipline means following rules.
  2. It is needed at home, in school and on the road.
  3. A disciplined student comes to school on time.
  4. He or she obeys parents and teachers.
  5. Discipline helps us to finish our work on time.
  6. The sun, the moon and the seasons also follow discipline.
  7. Players and soldiers cannot succeed without discipline.
  8. Discipline makes our life orderly and happy.
  9. A country grows when its people are disciplined.
  10. We should learn discipline from childhood.

Short essay on Discipline

For classes 4 to 6 · 129 words

Discipline means living by rules and doing the right thing at the right time, even when nobody is watching. Nature itself is the best example: the sun rises on time, the seasons come in order and the planets keep to their paths. In our daily life, discipline is needed everywhere. At home it means getting up on time, keeping our things in place and respecting elders. In school it means being punctual, paying attention and doing homework regularly. On the road it means following traffic rules. Soldiers, sportspersons and scientists all owe their success to strict discipline. Discipline should not come from fear of punishment. The best discipline is self-discipline, which comes from within. A disciplined student saves time, earns trust and moves steadily towards his or her goal.

Long essay on Discipline

For classes 7 to 10 · 374 words

The word discipline comes from a word that means "to learn". Discipline is the training that teaches us to control ourselves and to follow rules. Many people think of it as punishment or as a list of don'ts. In truth, it is the key that opens the door to success and freedom.

Look at nature. The sun never takes a holiday. Day follows night, and summer follows spring. Rivers flow within their banks; when they break their banks, there is a flood. The whole universe runs on order. If nature needs discipline, human life needs it even more.

For a student, discipline begins at home. Getting up at a fixed time, bathing, eating without fuss, keeping the school bag ready at night and going to bed on time are its first lessons. In school, a disciplined student reaches before the bell, stands quietly in the assembly, listens in class, raises a hand before speaking and finishes homework on the day it is given. Such a student does not need to study all night before examinations, because the work has been done little by little through the year.

Discipline matters in every field. An army without discipline is only a crowd. A cricket team may have great players, but it wins only when each one follows the captain's plan. A factory, a hospital, even a kitchen cannot run without order. On the road, one driver who jumps a red light can cause the death of many. A country where people stand in queues, pay taxes, keep public places clean and obey the law progresses quickly.

There are two kinds of discipline. One is forced on us from outside, through rules and punishment. It works only as long as someone is watching. The other is self-discipline. It comes from understanding why a rule exists. A self-disciplined person does the right thing even when alone. This is the discipline that lasts.

Discipline does not kill freedom or joy. A kite flies high because it is tied to a string; cut the string, and it falls. In the same way, discipline holds us steady so that we can rise. The habits formed in childhood stay for life, so the school years are the best time to learn it.

10 lines on Discipline in Hindi

  6. सूरज, चाँद और ऋतुएँ भी अनुशासन में चलते हैं।

  8. अनुशासन से जीवन सुखी और व्यवस्थित बनता है।

Points to write your own essay

Do not learn the essay by heart. Remember these points, in this order, and write them in your own sentences.

  1. Meaning of discipline
  2. Discipline in nature
  3. Discipline at home and in school
  4. In the army, sports, on the road, in a nation
  5. Self-discipline; discipline and freedom
